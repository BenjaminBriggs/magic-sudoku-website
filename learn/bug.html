BUG+1

Every empty square has two notes except one with three; the number that appears three times in that square's row, column and house goes there, or the puzzle would have two answers.

Worked example

BUG+1 in Action

How to spot it

  1. 1Late in a puzzle, check whether every empty square has two notes but one, which has three.
  2. 2In that square's row, find the note that appears three times. Every other note appears twice.
  3. 3Put that number in the square.

What it does

Places a number. What follows is usually easy.
