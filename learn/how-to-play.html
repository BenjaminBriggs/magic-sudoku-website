How to Play Sudoku

Sudoku is a number puzzle with only three rules. Learn them here, then move on to the techniques that help you solve any puzzle.

The Rules

The grid is 9 cells wide and 9 cells tall, split into nine 3×3 boxes called houses. Each cell holds one number from 1 to 9.

Rules at a Glance

Rows

Each of the 9 rows contains 1 to 9 exactly once.

Columns

Each of the 9 columns contains 1 to 9 exactly once.

Houses

Each of the 9 houses (3×3 boxes) contains 1 to 9 exactly once.

That's it. There's no maths involved. The numbers could just as easily be letters or colours; what matters is that each one appears once per row, column, and house.

Your First Puzzle

Getting started

  • Start with rows, columns, or houses that are already nearly full
  • For each empty cell, ask which numbers are still possible
  • When only one number fits, place it and look again

Good habits

  • Don't guess: every number can be found with logic
  • Use pencil marks to note the candidates for a cell
  • If you get stuck, move to a different part of the grid
