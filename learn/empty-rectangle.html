Empty Rectangle

A number whose places in one house all sit in one row and one column of it, but not all in one line, working with a row or column that doesn't cross the house and has only two places for it, to rule it out of one square.

Worked example

Empty Rectangle in Action

How to spot it

  1. 1Pick a number and find a house where its places all sit in one row and one column of the house.
  2. 2Look for a row outside the house where the number has only two places, one of them in that column.
  3. 3Cross the number off the square where the other place's column meets that row of the house.
  4. 4It works the same with a column outside the house, lined up with the house's row.

What it does

Crosses a note off one square.
