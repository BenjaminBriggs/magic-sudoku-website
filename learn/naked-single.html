Naked Single

A square with only one number left that fits, because its row, column and house already use the other eight.

Worked example

Naked Single in Action

How to spot it

  1. 1Pick an empty square. One in a nearly full row, column or house is a good start.
  2. 2Look along its row and column, and round its house. Every number there is ruled out.
  3. 3If eight numbers are ruled out, the ninth goes in the square.
  4. 4With notes, look for a square with only one note left.

What it does

Places a number. Near the end of a puzzle, most squares fill this way.
