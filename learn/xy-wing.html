XY-Wing

Three squares with two notes each, a pivot and two wings, where whatever the pivot turns out to be, one wing gets the same number, so any square that sees both wings can't have it.

Worked example

XY-Wing in Action

How to spot it

  1. 1Look for squares with only two notes.
  2. 2Pick one as the pivot. Find two squares it can see, each sharing a different one of its notes.
  3. 3Check that the two wings' other notes are the same number.
  4. 4Cross that number off any square that sees both wings.

What it does

Crosses notes off, usually one or two, often leaving a single behind.

XYZ-Wing

W-Wing

Expert

Two squares with the same two notes, linked through a row, column or house where one of those numbers has only two places; one of the squares must hold the other number, so any square that sees both can't.
