Privacy Policy

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Magic Sudoku ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our Magic Sudoku mobile application and website.

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Information We Collect

We collect the most minimal information to provide and improve our service:

  • Game progress and settings (stored locally on your device and optionally synced through iCloud if you choose to enable this feature)
  • If you contact us for support, we may collect your email address and any information you provide about your issue

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain our service
  • To save your game progress and settings
  • To respond to your support requests

Data Storage and Security

Game data is stored locally on your device. If you enable iCloud synchronization, your game data will be stored in your personal iCloud account, which is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information.

Third-Party Services

Our app uses Apple's Game Center for leaderboards and achievements. The use of this service is subject to Apple's privacy policy.

Without a subscription, the app may show a small banner recommending another independent app, delivered by Kickstart Exchange. These are not targeted adverts and do not track you:

  • To choose a recommendation, the app sends only app-level details: the app and SDK versions, platform, and App Store country. It does not send anything that identifies you or your device.
  • Kickstart Exchange records whether a recommendation was shown or tapped, without identifying you or your device, and keeps these records for about 30 days.
  • No data is used to track you across other apps or websites.
  • Magic Sudoku Unlimited subscribers never see these recommendations.

We do not share your personal information with any other third parties.

Your Rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us at support@magic-sudoku.com.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@magic-sudoku.com.
