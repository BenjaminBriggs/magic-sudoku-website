Frequently Asked Questions

How do I sync my progress across devices?

Magic Sudoku automatically syncs your game progress through iCloud. Make sure you're signed in to the same iCloud account on all your devices and have iCloud enabled for the app in your device settings.

What are Magic Mode hints?

Magic Mode provides intelligent assistance without solving the puzzle for you. It highlights patterns and techniques that you can apply to solve the current situation, helping you learn advanced solving methods.

How do I customize the number styles?

Go to Settings > Appearance > Number Style to choose from our collection of international numeral systems and custom designs.

Is Magic Sudoku available on Android?

Currently, Magic Sudoku is only available for iOS devices including iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Vision Pro.
