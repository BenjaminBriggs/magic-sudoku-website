Puzzle Submissions

You can send a puzzle you made in Magic Sudoku to us for review with Submit for Review. These terms apply to every puzzle you submit. Sharing a puzzle as a link, picture or PDF doesn't submit it.

What's sent

When you submit a puzzle, the app sends us:

  • The puzzle's starting numbers, a fingerprint of its solution (not the solution itself), and its difficulty as the app rated it.
  • The title, author name, instructions, solved note and link you gave it. The title and author name are required; the rest are optional.
  • Which clues you placed yourself and which, if any, the app chose for you.
  • An identifier for your iCloud account that Apple's CloudKit attaches to the submission. It's the same for every puzzle you submit, and it isn't your name, email address or Apple Account.

Submissions are stored in Magic Sudoku's public CloudKit database, run by Apple. See our Privacy Policy for more.
